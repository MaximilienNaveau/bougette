# Bougette

Bougette manages several budgets at once, individual and shared, for a group of people who each keep the data on their own devices.

## People and devices

**Member**:
A person who has access to one or more budgets. Members have no family roles: a budget can be shared by a couple, flatmates or friends alike.
_Avoid_: User, account, participant, parent, child

**Role**:
What a member may do in one budget: **Admin** (manages members and roles), **Editor** (changes lines and transactions) or **Reader** (views only). (fr: rôle)
_Avoid_: Permission, right, owner

**Device**:
One installation of the app, identified by its own key, belonging to exactly one member.
_Avoid_: Client, node, peer (when talking about the domain)

**Display name**:
How a member appears to the other members of their budgets, e.g. "Maximilien". (fr: nom affiché)
_Avoid_: Username (that belongs to a server), nickname

**Recovery phrase**:
A secret only the member knows, generated by the app or chosen by them, that unlocks their encrypted backup after they lose all their devices. Daily use never asks for it. (fr: phrase de récupération)
_Avoid_: Password, PIN, master password

**Server**:
An always-online device that keeps encrypted changes for devices that are offline, and each member's encrypted backup, without being able to read them.
_Avoid_: Relay (an iroh relay only forwards traffic), backend, cloud

**Server operator**:
The person who runs a server and decides which devices may use it. They can always restore someone's access to the server, never to the content of a budget. (fr: opérateur du serveur)
_Avoid_: Server admin, Admin (that's a role in a budget), host

**Username**:
A member's name on one server, chosen when the server operator lets them in, e.g. `max` on datcat.fr. (fr: nom d'utilisateur)
_Avoid_: Login, account, display name

**Invitation**:
A single-use, expiring link or QR code that lets someone join a budget, or a server, or both. (fr: invitation)
_Avoid_: Invite code, token

**Notification**:
A message inside the app, collected behind a bell with a counter, about something a member should know, e.g. an amount they entered that someone else overwrote. Never a system push notification. (fr: notification)
_Avoid_: Alert, push, message

## Budgets

**Budget**:
A plan of incomes and costs, and the transactions that actually happened, tracked together by one or more members in a single currency. It is not a bank account. An archived budget is read-only for everyone until it is restored or deleted.
_Avoid_: Wallet, account, envelope

**Category**:
A label grouping lines and transactions of a budget, e.g. Housing or Energy. Each budget has its own list. (fr: catégorie)
_Avoid_: Tag, type, group

**Line**:
A planned, recurring income or cost of a budget, e.g. electricity, rent or a salary, with a category, an amount, a schedule and the dates it is valid between. (fr: poste)
_Avoid_: Item, entry, expense (an expense is something that actually happened)

**Revision**:
A line's amount and schedule from a given date on, until the next revision. Changing a line from a date adds a revision; the months before keep the old one. (fr: révision)
_Avoid_: Version, change, update

**Schedule**:
When a line's amount is due: monthly on a given day, every N months, yearly on a date, weekly, or once (one-off). E.g. €120 every two months on the 5th, or €480 every March. (fr: échéancier)
_Avoid_: Recurrence, frequency (only one part of it)

**Smoothed amount**:
A recurring line's amount spread evenly over a chosen period (week, month, year…), whatever its schedule, e.g. €480 a year shown as €40 a month. One-off lines are not smoothed. (fr: montant lissé)
_Avoid_: Average, prorated amount

**Pot**:
Money set aside inside a budget, optionally towards a goal (e.g. €1,200 for a bike by June). Its balance is what has been allocated to it minus the transactions paid from it, which do not count against the month's plan. An investment is a pot too. (fr: cagnotte)
_Avoid_: Envelope, savings account, fund, project

**Allocation**:
A planned cost line that moves money from a budget into one of its pots, e.g. €100 a month to "Bike". (fr: dotation)
_Avoid_: Transfer, saving, contribution (that's money from a member)

**Asset**:
Something of lasting value bought through a budget, e.g. a rental flat or the house a household lives in, with a value its members update by hand and possibly a loan. (fr: bien)
_Avoid_: Property (too narrow), investment (a pot can be an investment too)

**Amortisation table**:
The month-by-month repayments of an asset's loan, split into interest and principal, with the principal still owed after each one. (fr: tableau d'amortissement)
_Avoid_: Schedule (that's a line's due dates), repayment plan

**Break-even date**:
The first date when selling an asset at its current value would give back everything put into it: value − principal still owed + what came in − what went out ≥ 0. Everything that went out counts (down payment, fees, repayments, insurance, taxes, management and works). (fr: date d'équilibre)
_Avoid_: Payback date, profitability date

**External income**:
An income line from outside the budget's members, e.g. a family allowance (CAF) paid into a shared budget. (fr: revenu externe)
_Avoid_: Subsidy, other income

**Plan**:
All the lines and contributions of a budget: what its members expect to happen. (fr: prévu)
_Avoid_: Forecast (that's something else), projection, estimate

**Actual**:
All the transactions of a budget: what really happened. (fr: réel)
_Avoid_: Real data, history

**Transaction**:
One amount of money that actually came in or went out on a given date, entered by hand or imported from a bank statement. (fr: opération)
_Avoid_: Movement, entry, payment

**Payer**:
Who pays a line or a transaction of a budget: the budget itself (its joint money) or one member, which makes an income their contribution and a cost their advance. What a member pays shows as a cost in their personal budget. (fr: payeur)
_Avoid_: Owner, author, spender

**Monthly balance**:
A budget's inflows minus its smoothed costs for one month: a surplus or a deficit. (fr: solde mensuel)
_Avoid_: Bank balance, result, profit

**Running balance**:
The monthly balances added up since the budget started, planned and actual. It is computed by the app, not read from a bank. (fr: solde cumulé)
_Avoid_: Account balance, reserve, savings

**Variance**:
The difference between plan and actual for a line or a budget over a period. (fr: écart)
_Avoid_: Delta, difference, overspend (only one direction)

**Forecast**:
The expected end-of-period outcome, extrapolated from the actual so far and the plan for what remains. (fr: projection)
_Avoid_: Plan, prediction, estimate

**Individual budget**:
A budget with exactly one member, private to them, holding their incomes (bonuses included) and personal costs. (fr: budget individuel)
_Avoid_: Personal account, private wallet

**Shared budget**:
A budget with more than one member, funded by contributions and advances from its members. (fr: budget commun)
_Avoid_: Joint account, common pot

**Split rule**:
A way of dividing a shared cost between members, e.g. in proportion to their incomes or in equal parts. (fr: clé de répartition)
_Avoid_: Ratio, quota, weighting

**Fair-split calculator**:
A standalone tool that suggests each member's contribution to a shared cost, using a split rule and figures entered on the spot, e.g. both incomes. By default, the shared cost is the smoothed total of an existing budget's cost lines. On a blank slate, it is a total typed in directly, worked out elsewhere. The incomes it is given are never stored or shared. (fr: calculateur de répartition)
_Avoid_: Simulator, wizard

**Contribution**:
Money a member puts into another budget, shared or not (e.g. a flat they rent out), usually every month. Each member sets their own amount, possibly following the fair-split calculator. Planned, it is one line of the receiving budget, paid by the member, that their personal budget shows as a cost line: entered from either budget, changed from either. (fr: versement)
_Avoid_: Deposit, top-up, transfer

**Advance**:
An expense of a shared budget that a member paid with their own money. The members see who paid what and when, and decide together whether and how to balance it. (fr: avance)
_Avoid_: Loan, IOU, reimbursable expense

**Settlement**:
A payment between members, or between a member and a shared budget, that the members decide on to even out what each has put in. Like a contribution, it is entered once in the shared budget and shows in the individual budget of the member involved. (fr: équilibrage)
_Avoid_: Refund, repayment, reconciliation
